# DESIGN.md — example

Template. A brand's character lives here; its values live in `tokens/`. Replace every
bracketed line. If a section stays empty after S2, that is a finding, not a formality —
the lock missed something.

## 1. Claim

[One sentence. What does every surface of this brand argue? Not a slogan — a claim a
layout can be held accountable to. If a section of a page cannot state how it encodes
this claim, the section is filler.]

## 2. Signature device

[The one visual mechanism that is this brand and nobody else's. Locked at S2. A device is
buildable and checkable — "warm and trustworthy" is a mood, "every blank is a writable
rule with 21pt of air above it" is a device.]

## 3. Tone and layer

[Which ENGINE §1 layer does the tone land in? A layer-3 expressive tone cannot later
descend into a dense print interior — record what this tone forecloses.]

## 4. Type

[Faces from ENGINE §5.1, roles, and the pairing argument. Serif vs sans is a real
contrast axis; sans vs sans is indecision.]

## 5. Colour

[What each role means — not what it looks like. A colour that names a document type,
a state, a voice. One accent hue; a second accent is a second brand.]

## 6. Motion choreography

[Prose, never tokens (ENGINE §3). What moves first, what follows, what never moves.
Example rule shape: the larger the change, the longer the duration.]

## 7. Voice

[How the brand writes. Sentence length, honorifics, what it refuses to say.]

## 8. Korean setting

[keep-all, leading floors, the matched Hangul faces — anything §5 leaves open that this
brand pins down.]

## 9. Forbidden

[The brand's own negative space, beyond the shared gates. What would look plausible on a
deadline and still be wrong here.]
